Technical Leadership & Engineering Services

Flexible senior support for product delivery, architecture, engineering teams, and application modernization.

I work with founders, technical leaders, agencies, and established companies that need experienced help without unnecessary process or inflated promises. Engagements can combine strategic guidance with hands-on delivery.

Fractional CTO & Technical Leadership#

Senior technical leadership adapted to the stage and reality of your company.

  • Technical strategy and pragmatic roadmaps
  • Software architecture and critical technical decisions
  • Engineering processes, team organization, and mentoring
  • Hiring support and technical assessment
  • Communication between product, business, and engineering

Early-Stage Products & POCs#

Turn an idea or uncertain product direction into something concrete enough to validate.

  • Product and technical discovery
  • POCs, prototypes, and MVP delivery
  • Technical foundations that can evolve with the product
  • Fast feedback loops and deliberately managed trade-offs

Legacy Application Modernization#

Improve a valuable existing application without defaulting to a risky full rewrite.

  • Codebase and architecture audits
  • Progressive refactoring and migration planning
  • Performance, reliability, and maintainability improvements
  • Testing strategy and reduction of technical debt

Senior Full-Stack Engineering#

Hands-on engineering support across frontend, backend, APIs, data, and cloud infrastructure.

  • Architecture and implementation
  • Integration with an existing engineering team
  • Complex feature delivery and problem solving
  • Backend, frontend, database, and infrastructure work

AI-Accelerated Engineering#

Use AI where it genuinely improves the work, while keeping experienced human judgment in control.

  • AI-assisted development workflows and codebase analysis
  • Internal automation and developer productivity
  • Responsible adoption of AI tools within engineering teams
  • Product AI features when they solve a real user problem

A practical engagement model#

The format depends on the problem: focused advisory sessions, an audit and action plan, fractional leadership, or hands-on delivery within your team. The first conversation is used to clarify the context, constraints, and most useful next step.
