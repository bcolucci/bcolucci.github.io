About

Experienced judgment, direct collaboration, hands-on execution.

My approach is pragmatic: understand the real constraint, make trade-offs visible, and leave both the product and the team stronger. I use coding agents daily for analysis, implementation, testing, refactoring, documentation, and automation. They shorten feedback loops; responsibility for architecture, quality, security, and business outcomes stays human.
